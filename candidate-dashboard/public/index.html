Rescheduled Interviews

Interview events rescheduled more than a couple times. Ashby has no reschedule history of its own, so this only counts reschedules this app has actually observed since it started tracking — it can't see further back than that.

Recently Sourced

Stale Candidates

Far enough past the normal thresholds that these have likely fallen through the cracks, not just freshly overdue.

Onsite Interviews Today

Today's final-round and executive interviews.

Interviewer Weekly Limits

Interviewers at or nearing their Ashby-configured weekly interview limit, for the current calendar week. Interviewers with no limit set never appear here. Not affected by the department/job/recruiter/ coordinator filter — that's on the Dashboard tab and doesn't apply here.

Interviewer Training

Interviewers currently in a pool's training path — shadow and reverse-shadow stages, with progress toward each stage's required interview count. Paused trainees are hidden by default.

Offers Awaiting Acceptance

The offer has been extended to the candidate — waiting on their decision.

Offers Not Yet Sent

The offer has been created but hasn't been sent to the candidate yet — usually still working through this org's internal approval chain, if it uses one.

Offers Signed

Offers the candidate has accepted in the last 7 days.
